Overlays, live off the lap

Same draw functions as the catalogue, fed by the car above. Brake spikes line up with the red sections of track.

How it works. Each circuit is a handful of control points, splined into a closed loop and resampled at even spacing. Curvature at every point gives a cornering speed limit; two passes over the loop propagate braking backwards and acceleration forwards, producing a speed profile. Longitudinal acceleration then falls out as throttle or brake, curvature becomes steering, and speed picks the gear. Adding a circuit means adding an array of points, which is why ten is not meaningfully harder than one.

Honesty note. Everything here is generated, including the values that would be telemetry on a real rig. That is fine for a showcase, but the site should say so plainly - a demo lap is demo data, and the DESIGN.md rule about never inferring one signal from another applies to live overlays, not to a simulator whose whole job is to be simulated.
