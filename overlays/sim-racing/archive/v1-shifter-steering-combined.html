The shifter is the most under-served input on stream. Nobody shows it, and it is the one that most obviously communicates skill. The mode switch above changes the simulation - H-pattern gets a full clutch and a slow throw, sequential gets a quick tap, paddles get neither.

Steering is harder to make interesting than pedals because it is one value, not three. The good options either add a second dimension - history, rate of change, predicted path - or lean fully into being a physical object on screen.

Combining all three is a layout problem, not a drawing problem. The question is always the same: what does a viewer glance at, and what do they study? Sizes here are deliberately varied, including one built for a tall case screen.
