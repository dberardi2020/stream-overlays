Overlay catalogue

Every tile declares where its data comes from. Nothing is derived from something else and presented as though it were measured.

DirectValues the rig reports over HID. Pedal axes, wheel axis, shifter buttons, paddle presses. Available today, no game integration.
InferredCounted from events rather than read. Only appears for sequential and paddle modes, where absolute gear has to be tracked and can drift.
TelemetryRequires the game. RPM, speed, and the gear the car is actually in. Shown here with stand-in values so you can judge the design.

On an H-pattern shifter each gear is its own button, so the gear and neutral are both directly readable - the gate knob below travels the actual H channels and genuinely passes through neutral mid-shift. Switch the mode to sequential or paddles and any tile showing an absolute gear flips to Inferred, because at that point you are counting shifts rather than reading a position.

Steering is a single axis, so everything here is direct. The interesting designs add a second dimension out of the same value - history, rate of change, distribution, or a recorded reference lap.

Layouts. Turn on "Direct input only" to see which of these you could ship this week versus which are waiting on a telemetry adapter.

The contract: every tile is draw(ctx, w, h, input, tel). Direct values arrive in input; anything from the game arrives in tel, which is null until a telemetry adapter exists. A tile that never touches tel runs on the rig alone. Click a tile to shortlist it.
